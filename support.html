Common questions

I started a session and locked my phone. Was that broken?

No. The Live Activity continues your session on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island. Locking the phone, switching apps, putting it down — all of these are normal interactions that Endured was built to expect. A session ends only when you tap End in the app.

What counts as a "broken" session?

In v1, no automatic action breaks a session — sessions are either finished deliberately (you tap End) or interrupted by the system (the app is force-quit). Endured preserves the broken state in its data model for a future addition: a "bail" affordance on the Live Activity that lets you explicitly stop a session you've decided not to finish.

Why don't I see streaks or daily totals?

Endured is built around the premise that the work is the point, not the metric. The History screen shows your sessions grouped by day with day totals; that's the most aggregation Endured does. If you want longer-term tracking of your focus time, Apple Health surfaces it as Mindful Minutes in the Mindfulness section.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Endured's data is included in standard iCloud device backups and iPhone-to-iPhone transfers. There is no manual export or sign-in. When you set up a new iPhone from a backup, your sessions come with it.

Can I delete my data?

Yes — uninstall Endured. Because all data is stored locally on your device, removing the app removes all of it, immediately and completely. There is no cloud copy and no account to delete.
