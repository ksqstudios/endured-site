Endured A focus session recorder

Privacy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

Endured does not collect any data about you. There is no account to create, and KSQ Studios runs no servers of its own. Your sessions sync across your devices through your own private iCloud, where only you can see them. We never receive a copy.

Where your sessions live

Every session you record, including its start time, end time, elapsed duration, and end reason, is stored in a local SwiftData database on your iPhone and synced across your devices through Apple's CloudKit. That sync uses the private database of your personal iCloud account: the data is encrypted, tied to your Apple ID, and reaches only the devices you are signed in on, such as your iPhone and iPad. KSQ Studios has no servers involved, cannot access it, and never receives a copy. If you sign out of iCloud, Endured keeps working with the sessions already on your device.

If you also have iCloud Backup enabled, this data may be included in your device backup, encrypted under your Apple ID, per your iCloud settings.

Apple Health

Endured can optionally write completed sessions to Apple Health as Mindful Session samples. This is opt-in: the first time a session ends, iOS will ask whether to allow Endured to write to Health. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Endured.

Endured only writes to Health. It never reads from it. The data Endured writes is governed thereafter by Apple's privacy practices, not ours.

No third-party services in the app

Endured the iOS app contains no third-party analytics SDKs, no tracking pixels, no advertising, and no telemetry. The only data that leaves the app is the iCloud sync described above, which goes to your own private iCloud, and the optional Health write you choose to allow.

This website

The endured.app website uses Plausible Analytics, a privacy-respecting analytics service that does not use cookies and does not collect personal data. You can read about how Plausible handles data at plausible.io/data-policy.

Children

Endured is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
