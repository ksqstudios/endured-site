Endured 2.1

A dark appearance, and a layout for iPhone Duo.

A dark appearance

Endured has been light only since it shipped. Cream paper is right at a desk in daylight and wrong at eleven at night, which is when a long session usually runs. The gradient still deepens as the session does, Bone through Iron, but the whole arc now sits where it belongs after dark.

Built for iPhone Duo

On the inner display, the session in progress sits beside the record of every session before it. Begin, the history, Insight and Settings stay to the left while the gradient deepens on the right. Nothing is hidden to make room for the moment.

And on iPad

The same change applies there. The record used to give way to a full-screen session; now it stays where you can see it. An honest record is more use beside the work than behind it.

Also in this release

Insight's time range control was rebuilt in Endured's own palette rather than the system's, and the launch screen no longer flashes white before the app appears.
